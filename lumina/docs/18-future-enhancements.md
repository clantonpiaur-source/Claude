# 18. Future Enhancements

Post-GA roadmap extending Lumina from a knowledge *repository* toward a knowledge *operating system* — AI that not only answers from documentation but actively maintains it.

---

## 18.1 Autonomous Knowledge Maintenance (Agentic)

- **Content drift detection:** agents monitor product changelogs, code, tickets, and analytics to flag/auto-draft updates when docs go stale.
- **Auto-generated release notes** from commits/PRs/issue trackers, routed through workflow for approval.
- **Self-healing links & references;** automatic redirect + cross-link maintenance.
- **Proactive gap-filling:** zero-result searches → auto-drafted articles for human review.

## 18.2 Advanced AI

- **Multi-agent workflows** (research → draft → review → publish) with human checkpoints.
- **Voice interface** — ask/answer by voice; audio article narration (TTS) + transcripts.
- **Multimodal understanding** — diagrams, screenshots, video content indexed and answerable.
- **Personalized answers** by role/skill level (difficulty-aware responses).
- **Fine-tuned/distilled per-tenant models** for cost + domain accuracy; continual eval-driven improvement.
- **Agent memory** — long-term org knowledge graph powering reasoning across documents.

## 18.3 Collaboration & Authoring

- **Real-time co-authoring with AI** as a live participant; inline "explain this change."
- **Whiteboard/canvas mode** (Miro-like) integrated with docs.
- **Video walkthroughs** auto-generated from articles (script + TTS + screen capture).
- **Approval-by-Slack/Teams** with rich actions.

## 18.4 Knowledge Graph & Semantics

- Entity/topic knowledge graph linking articles, products, teams, and terms.
- Ontology/taxonomy management; automatic concept linking and glossary.
- Graph-augmented RAG (GraphRAG) for multi-hop questions.

## 18.5 Delivery & Experience

- **Personalized help centers** (adaptive content by user segment/behavior).
- **In-product contextual help** SDK (surface relevant docs inside any app).
- **AI chat widget** embeddable anywhere with deflection analytics.
- **Interactive tutorials / product tours** authored in Lumina.
- **Offline-first native desktop app.**

## 18.6 Enterprise & Compliance

- **On-prem / air-gapped** deployment with fully self-hosted models.
- **Advanced data governance:** classification, DLP, sensitivity labels, retention automation.
- **Customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK)** everywhere; confidential computing.
- Additional certifications: **HIPAA, FedRAMP, ISO 27701**.
- **Sovereign cloud** options per region/government.

## 18.7 Analytics & Intelligence

- **Predictive content analytics** (which docs will go stale, deflection forecasting).
- **ROI dashboards** (support cost saved, time-to-answer, self-service rate).
- **Content strategy assistant** recommending what to write next from demand signals.
- **Sentiment analysis** on feedback and search intent.

## 18.8 Ecosystem & Extensibility

- **Marketplace growth:** paid extensions, themes, integrations; partner program + rev-share.
- **Workflow/automation builder** (visual, no-code) beyond built-in rules.
- **Public API GA v2** with GraphQL subscriptions, bulk, and streaming exports.
- **Data connectors** for warehouses, BI, and data lakes.

## 18.9 Platform

- **Edge-native rendering** improvements; even lower TTFB globally.
- **Cost-aware AI autopilot** dynamically choosing models to hit quality/cost targets.
- **Green/efficiency mode** — carbon-aware scheduling of batch AI/indexing.

---

## Prioritization Framework

Enhancements are scored on **customer demand × strategic value × effort × risk**; sequenced quarterly; each ships behind flags with evals and analytics to validate impact before GA. The north star remains constant: **make organizational knowledge instantly findable, trustworthy, and self-maintaining.**
