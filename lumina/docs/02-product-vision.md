# 2. Product Vision

## 2.1 Vision Statement

> **To make every organization's collective knowledge instantly findable, trustworthy, and actionable — for every employee, customer, and machine.**

Lumina becomes the *system of record and system of answers* for organizational knowledge: authored once, governed continuously, delivered everywhere, and answered by AI that cites its sources.

## 2.2 Mission

Give teams a single AI-native platform where knowledge is easy to write, impossible to lose, effortless to find, and safe to trust — replacing the fragmented sprawl of wikis, help centers, and doc tools.

## 2.3 Product Principles

1. **Content is the interface.** The UI recedes; knowledge and answers lead.
2. **Trust is a feature.** Every AI answer is grounded, cited, and auditable. Governance is default-on.
3. **Author once, deliver everywhere.** Reusable snippets, variables, conditional content, and multi-format delivery (web, PDF, API, AI).
4. **Findability is the product.** If it can't be found, it doesn't exist — search and AI are core, not add-ons.
5. **Enterprise-ready from line one.** Multi-tenancy, security, compliance, and scale are architectural, not afterthoughts.
6. **Open & extensible.** APIs, webhooks, SDKs, and a marketplace so Lumina fits any stack.

## 2.5 Value Proposition by Buyer

- **VP Support:** ↓ ticket volume via self-service + AI deflection; ↑ CSAT.
- **VP Engineering:** best-in-class developer portal & API docs; Git-style versioning.
- **VP Ops/HR:** SOP libraries, policies, training manuals with approval workflows and expiry.
- **CISO:** SSO/SCIM, RBAC/ABAC, audit, residency, SOC 2 / ISO 27001 alignment.
- **CIO:** consolidation of 3–5 tools into one; measurable ROI via analytics.

## 2.7 Product Pillars → Feature Themes

1. **Author** — editor, collaboration, reuse, workflow, versioning.
2. **Organize** — categories, metadata, health, SEO, lifecycle.
3. **Deliver** — public/private/internal sites, branding, domains, i18n.
4. **Find** — hybrid & semantic search, related/suggested, instant.
5. **Assist** — AI writing, translation, RAG chat, governance.
6. **Measure** — analytics, feedback, content gaps, productivity.
7. **Govern** — RBAC/ABAC, SSO, audit, compliance, residency.
8. **Extend** — APIs, webhooks, SDKs, integrations, marketplace.

## 2.8 Long-Term North Star

Lumina evolves from a knowledge *repository* into a knowledge *operating system*: an AI agent layer that not only answers from documentation but **maintains** it — detecting drift, proposing updates, generating release notes, and keeping every organization's truth current automatically (see §18 Future Enhancements).
